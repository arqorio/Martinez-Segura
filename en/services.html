What we do

Surveying, engineering and design services

From boundary surveys to construction control. Precise measurement with total station and GPS, cadastral-valid drawings, and support at every stage — across Nicaragua and Central America.

Surveying

01

Cadastral survey

Boundary and land measurement valid before the cadastral registry, with a full drawing set and support through the process.

02

Topographic survey

Contours, profiles and planimetry of the site as a base for design and engineering.

03

Construction layout & control

Setting out of axes and levels, structure verticality and topographic control on site.

04

Geodetic control points

Georeferenced GNSS control points to tie your project to the national geodetic network.

Engineering & design

05

Architectural & structural design

Complete construction drawings and calculation reports, ready for building permits.

06

BIM modeling & coordination

Coordinated 3D model, clash detection and quantity take-off — methodology proven on large-scale projects such as the AIFA airport.

07

Roadway & earthwork design

Design of streets, parking and accesses with cut-and-fill volume calculations.

08

Costs & budgets

Itemized budgets and quantity take-offs so you know your investment before you build.

Where we work

Field surveying across Nicaragua's 12 departments. For Mexico, Guatemala, Costa Rica and El Salvador we provide design, BIM and project management remotely, with field execution carried out by licensed local partners.
